# M7 journal — Observability hardening: correlation + TRACEPARENT propagation

**Branch:** `feat/observability-m7` off `main@81b5eea`
**Goal:** Turn three independent, off-by-default signals (slog logs, Prometheus metrics, OTel traces) into a *correlated* story — a single `trace_id` joins a log line → its span → a metric exemplar — and close ADR 0015's "no cross-process trace propagation" gap.
**ADRs:** [0026](../docs/adr/0026-traceparent-wire-propagation.md) (TRACEPARENT wire), [0027](../docs/adr/0027-correlated-telemetry.md) (correlated telemetry)

## Scope note — M7 vs M7.5
This milestone is the **code + wire** half: instrumentation depth, log/trace
correlation, TRACEPARENT propagation. The **provisioned Grafana LGTM stack**
(docker-compose, OTel Collector, Tempo, Loki, Alloy, dashboards, `alerts.yml`)
is a separate config-only follow-on, **M7.5**, and is not in this branch.

## What I did
- **Metrics — 11 new series** (`internal/metrics/registry.go`), all nil-safe on the existing pattern: `ack_total`, `nack_total`, `consumer_lag_messages` (the roadmap's lag exporter), `dlq_total{trigger}`, `delayed_pending`, `retention_segments/bytes_reclaimed_total`, `partition_latest_msgid`, `wal_segments`, `command_errors_total{verb,code}`, `publish_failure_total`. Wired at their call sites across `broker.go`, `partition.go`, `redelivery.go`, `retention.go`, `dlq.go`, `session.go`.
- **WAL-append exemplar** — `ObserveWALAppendExemplar` attaches a `trace_id` exemplar to `toymq_wal_append_seconds` (metric→trace pivot). Added `wal.Log.Head()` for the lag computation.
- **Log/trace correlation** — new `internal/logging` package: a `slog.Handler` wrapper that injects `trace_id`/`span_id` from the ctx span. Wired into `buildLogger`; traced log sites moved to `*Context` calls.
- **TRACEPARENT wire (ADR 0026)** — `TraceparentCommand` + `parseTraceparent` in `proto`; W3C propagator helpers (`ContextWithTraceparent` / `TraceparentFromContext`) in `tracing`; the session stashes a pending traceparent and parents the next PUB/SUB span on the caller's span (`AckCtx`/`NackCtx`/`dlqMoveCtx` added for the ctx plumbing).
- **New spans** — `broker.ack`, `broker.nack`, `broker.dlq_move`.
- **Client opt-in** — `pkg/client.WithTraceparentFunc`, an **otel-free** callback (respects ADR 0013's stdlib-only client) that prepends the TRACEPARENT line before PUB/SUB.
- **Owned risk test** — `internal/integration/m7_test.go` (passes `-race`): propagation makes `broker.publish` a child of the client span; a log line carries the matching `trace_id`; a no-TRACEPARENT client is unchanged and its span is a root; `consumer_lag_messages` = head − lastAcked after a partial ack.

## Problems I faced
**End-to-end producer→consumer stitching is not free.** The design sketched a MSG `TRACEPARENT` continuation, but the client's MSG parser requires exactly 5 fields, so a 6th token breaks pre-M7 clients — and genuine stitching across the *durable* log needs the producer's trace context **persisted in the WAL record** (delivery can be minutes later, or post-restart), which is a record-format change with ADR 0018 determinism concerns. I scoped the continuation **out** and documented it as a future item in ADR 0026; producer→broker propagation (the tested, high-value half) ships.

**Keeping otel out of `pkg/client`.** ADR 0013 makes the client stdlib-only, but propagation needs the W3C header. Resolved with `WithTraceparentFunc(func(ctx) (tp, ts string))` — a callback the caller wires to `tracing.TraceparentFromContext`, so otel never enters the client's dependency closure.

**Correlation only fires on `*Context` log calls.** The correlation handler reads the span from the record's context, so a bare `slog.Info` in a traced path silently misses the ids. Had to convert the hot-path traced log sites (`consumer subscribed`, dead-lettering) to `slog.InfoContext`; the test asserts the join to catch regressions.

**`delayed_pending` across restart.** A live counter can't cleanly survive a restart (a delayed record published before the restart fires with no matching increment). Made it best-effort/in-process with a clamp-at-0 decrement — matching the ADR 0024 precedent for the DLQ attempt count — rather than pretending it's durable.

**`command_errors_total` verb label.** On a parse failure the Command is nil, so there's no structured verb. Recovered it from the error text (which echoes the offending verb) for a low-cardinality label; documented as an approximation in ADR 0027.

## What could have been done better
- The MSG-continuation dead-end could have been caught at design time by checking the client MSG parser's field-count strictness before promising the continuation in the spec.
- The `*Context`-only correlation constraint is a footgun for future log sites; a lint or a house rule ("traced paths log with `*Context`") would prevent silent misses.
